## Project Title
Superstore Sales EDA — Discount Impact & Profitability Analysis

## Tools
BigQuery SQL | Tableau | Google Sheets

## Business Question
Is the company's discount strategy hurting profitability —
and which categories, regions, and segments are most at risk?

## Key Findings
- Over-discounting destroyed $125K (43.6% of total profit)
- Furniture: 32% of revenue, only 6.4% of profit
- Central region: lowest margin (7.92%), highest avg discount (24%)
- South: sales grew 31.5% in 2014 while profit fell 49.9%
